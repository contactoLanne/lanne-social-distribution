Creator publishing workspace

Review once. Publish with control.

Lanne Social Distribution helps creators prepare, review, and send original short-form video content to channels they have explicitly authorized.

See the publishing workflow How data is handled

Creator control first

Connected platforms are authorized by the account owner. TikTok posts are reviewed before submission, including caption, privacy, and available interaction settings.

The workspace does not sell personal data and does not publish to an account that has not been authorized.

What the workspace does

Organize owned content

Prepare original short-form videos and the metadata needed for distribution.

Review platform settings

See account information and choose the settings available for the connected destination.

Track delivery

Record publishing status and platform identifiers so creators can avoid accidental duplicate submissions.

Publishing flow

01
Connect

The creator authorizes a supported account through the platform's official authorization flow.

02
Prepare

The workspace prepares the selected video, caption, and publishing metadata.

03
Review

For TikTok, the creator reviews account details, manually chooses privacy and interaction settings, and confirms submission.

04
Publish & track

The workspace sends the approved content through the official API and records the publishing result.

TikTok publishing

Before a TikTok Direct Post is initialized, the workspace retrieves the latest Creator Info, displays the available privacy choices, respects unavailable interaction settings, and asks the creator to review the post before it is submitted.
