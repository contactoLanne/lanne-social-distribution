Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Lanne Social Distribution is a workflow used by an authorized creator to distribute original social media content to connected platforms.

Information processed

The workflow may process video files, captions, post metadata, platform account identifiers, authorization tokens, and publishing status information as necessary to perform authorized content distribution.

Purpose

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, prepare content, publish content, and record publishing results.

Data sharing

Personal data is not sold. Information is shared only with connected platforms as required to perform actions requested by the authorized account owner.

Retention and access

Authorization can be revoked through the relevant platform. Locally stored workflow data may be removed by the account owner.
