Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Lanne Social Distribution is a creator publishing workspace used to prepare, review, and distribute original short-form content to accounts that a creator has explicitly authorized.

Information processed

The service may process video files, captions, publishing metadata, platform account identifiers, authorization tokens, creator information returned by connected platforms, privacy and interaction choices, and publishing status information when needed to provide an authorized publishing workflow.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, display current account and publishing options, prepare creator-selected content, submit content after the required creator review, prevent duplicate submissions, and record publishing results.

TikTok data and publishing

When TikTok is connected, the service may use authorized TikTok API data to identify the connected creator, display current publishing options, and perform a Direct Post after the creator reviews the post and submits their choices. TikTok authorization tokens are used only for the connected account and authorized scopes.

Data sharing

Personal data is not sold. Information is shared with connected platforms only as necessary to carry out the publishing actions requested by the authorized creator or to provide the connected feature.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Creators can revoke platform authorization through the relevant platform. Locally stored publishing records and workflow data can be deleted at the creator's request. Authorization tokens are retained only as needed to keep an authorized connection operating.

Security

Access credentials and authorization tokens are intended to be stored server-side and are not displayed publicly. The service uses official platform authorization and API flows for connected features.

Contact

For privacy, access, or deletion requests, contact lauanne.correia@gmail.com.
